BITCOIN FOR TRAVELERS

Use Bitcoin while you travel

Bitcoin acceptance is voluntary. Learn the basics, confirm before paying and always keep another payment method.

₿
👋

Ask before ordering

Look for a Bitcoin or Lightning sign and confirm that the business is accepting it today.

⚡

Use Lightning for small payments

Confirm the amount, currency and recipient, then wait for the payment-success screen.

💵

Keep a USD backup

Cash and cards remain essential because Bitcoin is not accepted everywhere.

🔐

Protect your wallet

Never share your recovery phrase, PIN or private keys. Do not let a stranger configure your wallet.

GET AROUND FOR LESS

El Salvador bus guide

Find practical public bus and tourist shuttle options for popular routes. Prices and times are estimates—always confirm before boarding.

💵 Carry small USD billsMost public buses are cash-only.
☀️ Travel during daylight

Leave early, especially when a trip requires transfers or serves rural areas.

🗣️ Confirm before boarding

Say or show your destination to the driver and ask where to get off.

🎒 Keep valuables close

Keep documents, phone and money with you—not on a roof rack or unattended.

Route numbers, stops, fares and departure times can change without notice. Confirm at your accommodation, terminal or with the driver on the day of travel.

Information sources: Spring Bus · CentroCoasting · Cartorux
