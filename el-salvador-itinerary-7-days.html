PERSONALIZED TRIP IDEAS

7 Days in El Salvador

Spend one week exploring the capital, volcanoes, archaeological sites, Ruta de las Flores and the La Libertad surf coast at a comfortable pace.

Personalize this itineraryOpen the interactive map

Suggested route

Day 1: Arrive and explore San Salvador

Settle in, explore a manageable section of the capital and enjoy a Salvadoran dinner.

Day 2: El Boquerón and city culture

Visit the volcano area in cooler morning weather, then choose a museum, viewpoint or the Historic Center.

Day 3: Santa Ana Volcano

Leave early and follow current park and guide requirements. Do not schedule a distant evening activity.

Day 4: Lake Coatepeque and archaeology

Enjoy the lake and choose Tazumal or Joya de Cerén depending on your route.

Day 5: Ruta de las Flores

Travel through selected towns such as Juayúa, Apaneca and Ataco, focusing on food, coffee and local culture.

Day 6: El Tunco, El Sunzal or El Zonte

Choose the beach community that matches your interests. Book instruction if you want to surf.

Day 7: La Libertad and departure buffer

Enjoy the coast while keeping enough time for airport traffic, car return or your shuttle.

Adjust it to your interests

Use the itinerary planner to prioritize beaches, surfing, mountains, food and coffee, culture, ruins, nightlife, horseback riding or 4×4 experiences. A relaxed pace works best if you prefer long meals and hotel time; an active pace adds more stops each day.

Before departure

Confirm attraction hours, reservations, weather, road conditions and travel time. Avoid crossing the entire country repeatedly: group nearby destinations and use one accommodation base for consecutive days when practical.
