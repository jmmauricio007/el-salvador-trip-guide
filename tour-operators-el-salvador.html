Checklist before booking

  • Confirm the exact business name and current tourism registration (RNT), where applicable.
  • Get the itinerary, inclusions, guide language and total price in writing.
  • Ask about vehicle or boat authorization, passenger insurance and the named driver or captain.
  • For surf, volcano, diving or adventure activities, confirm trained guides and suitable equipment.
  • Agree on pickup location, return time, currency, payment schedule and receipt.
  • Read the cancellation, refund and bad-weather policy.
  • Save a working emergency contact and share the itinerary with someone you trust.

Warning signs

Pause before paying if an operator pressures you to send the full amount to an unrelated personal account, refuses to provide a written itinerary or price, avoids questions about registration or insurance, promises unsafe access, or cannot be reached through current contact details.
