El Cuco Beach Travel Guide

El Cuco is a broad Pacific beach and a practical base for exploring eastern El Salvador, from Las Flores surf break to Punta Mango and the Gulf of Fonseca.

Best forBeach · Seafood · Relaxing
Suggested stay1–3 nights
From San MiguelAbout 1–1.5 hours

Beach time

The long shore is ideal for walking and sunsets. Ask locally about currents and safe swimming areas because conditions and lifeguard coverage vary.

Where to stay and eat

Choose between village lodging and surf hotels toward Las Flores. Confirm air conditioning, parking, beach access and restaurant hours before booking.

Getting there

Route 320 commonly connects San Miguel and El Cuco. Confirm the terminal and final departure; private transport is easier with boards or a late arrival.

Practical tips

Carry sun protection, small USD bills and a backup payment method. Arrange transport before evening when staying outside the village center.

Nearby experiences

Las Flores, Punta Mango, Chirilagua, San Miguel and Conchagua.
