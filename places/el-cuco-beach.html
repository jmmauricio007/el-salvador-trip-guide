El Cuco Beach

El Cuco is a broad, relaxed Pacific beach and the main tourism base for the San Miguel coast. Its dark sand, warm water, seafood restaurants and proximity to Las Flores make it practical for families, couples and surf travelers.

Beach time

The long shoreline is good for walking and sunsets. Ocean conditions can change, and lifeguard coverage may be limited; ask locally about currents and safe swimming areas before entering.

Where to stay

Choose between simple lodging near the village and surf-oriented hotels toward Las Flores. Confirm air conditioning, hot water, parking, beach access and restaurant hours before booking.

Getting there

Route 320 commonly connects San Miguel and El Cuco. Confirm the boarding terminal and last return departure. A private transfer is more convenient with boards, luggage or a late arrival.

Practical planning

Carry sun protection, small cash and a backup payment method. Avoid leaving valuables on the sand, and arrange transportation before evening if staying outside the village center.

Nearby experiences

Las Flores, Punta Mango, Chirilagua, San Miguel and Conchagua.
