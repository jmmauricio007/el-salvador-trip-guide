Alegría Lagoon Travel Guide

Alegría Lagoon sits inside the Tecapa volcanic crater near one of eastern El Salvador’s most attractive highland towns, combining crater scenery, gardens, cafés and cooler weather.

The lagoon

The water and access conditions can vary with weather and volcanic activity. Respect barriers and local instructions, and do not enter the water unless officials clearly permit it.

Alegría town

Combine the crater with the central park, nurseries, viewpoints, coffee and local restaurants. A night in town creates a slower visit.

Getting there

A car, driver or organized tour provides the simplest access. Roads climb into the mountains, and public transport details should be confirmed locally.

Practical tips

Bring shoes with grip, sun and rain protection, water and a light layer. Fog and rain can reduce visibility quickly.

Nearby experiences

Alegría town, Berlín, Santiago de María, Tecapa Volcano and Jiquilisco Bay.
