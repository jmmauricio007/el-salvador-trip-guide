Punta Roca Surf Guide

Punta Roca is El Salvador’s internationally renowned right-hand point break. Its long, powerful walls and rocky bottom make it a destination for experienced surfers rather than beginners.

Who should surf it

Punta Roca is powerful, fast and crowded when conditions are good. Advanced ability, fitness and confident board control are essential.

Entry and safety

Rocky access, currents and local etiquette require care. Speak with a reputable local surf guide before entering and never paddle out beyond your ability.

Getting there

The break is beside Puerto de La Libertad and is easiest to reach by car, driver or coastal bus followed by a short local connection.

Where to stay

Stay around La Libertad, El Tunco, El Sunzal or El Zonte. Compare distance, secure board storage, parking and early breakfast before booking.

Nearby experiences

Puerto de La Libertad, El Tunco, El Sunzal, El Zonte and Walter Thilo Deininger Park.
