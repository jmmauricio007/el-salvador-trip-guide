El Zonte Travel Guide

El Zonte is a relaxed Pacific beach community known for consistent surf, a growing collection of cafés and hotels, and the local project that became known internationally as Bitcoin Beach.

What to do

Book a surf lesson, walk the rocky shoreline at sunset, visit local cafés and use El Zonte as a quieter base for exploring the Surf City coast.

Surf and swimming

Several breaks serve different levels, but rocks, currents and swell can be challenging. Ask a qualified local instructor where it is safe to enter that day.

Getting there

Coastal buses connect La Libertad with El Zonte, while a pre-booked transfer is easier with luggage or surfboards. Confirm the last return before leaving.

Money and practical tips

Some businesses may accept Bitcoin or Lightning, but acceptance is voluntary and changes. Confirm before ordering and always carry USD and another payment option.

Nearby experiences

El Sunzal, El Tunco, Punta Roca, Mizata and Puerto de La Libertad.
