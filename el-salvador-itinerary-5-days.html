5 Days in El Salvador

A balanced five-day plan with the capital, western volcanoes, colorful Ruta de las Flores towns and the Pacific coast.

Personalize this itineraryOpen the interactive map

Suggested route

Day 1: San Salvador

Begin with the Historic Center, museums or El Boquerón depending on arrival time.

Day 2: Santa Ana Volcano and Coatepeque

Plan an early volcano excursion, then relax beside Lake Coatepeque.

Day 3: Ruta de las Flores

Explore Juayúa, Apaneca and Ataco without trying to rush every town. Prioritize coffee, murals, food and viewpoints.

Day 4: Archaeology or nature

Choose Tazumal and Joya de Cerén for culture, or spend more time in the western highlands.

Day 5: Surf City

Finish along the La Libertad coast with a beach, surf lesson, seafood and sunset.

Adjust it to your interests

Use the itinerary planner to prioritize beaches, surfing, mountains, food and coffee, culture, ruins, nightlife, horseback riding or 4×4 experiences. A relaxed pace works best if you prefer long meals and hotel time; an active pace adds more stops each day.

Before departure

Confirm attraction hours, reservations, weather, road conditions and travel time. Avoid crossing the entire country repeatedly: group nearby destinations and use one accommodation base for consecutive days when practical.
