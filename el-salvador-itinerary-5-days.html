Home of the Right Surf: 5-day itinerary

A coast-to-city route inspired by the official Ministry of Tourism itinerary. It mixes surfing, coastal forest, a mountain viewpoint, city culture and western marine life without requiring a long cross-country drive every day.

Five-day route

Day 1: El Tunco

Begin in this walkable surf community with its emblematic rock, nearby breaks, restaurants and evening atmosphere. New surfers should book a lesson and follow local guidance around rocky entries.

Show El Tunco on our map →

Day 2: Walter Thilo Deininger Adventure Park

Exchange the beach for trails through a coastal dry-forest reserve near Puerto de La Libertad. Ask the park directly about current access, guides and adventure activities before arriving.

Show the park on our map →

Day 3: Peñón de Comasagua

Travel into the Cordillera del Bálsamo for a scenic hike to a rocky viewpoint overlooking green ridges and, in clear weather, the Pacific horizon. Use suitable footwear and avoid exposed sections during storms.

Show Peñón de Comasagua on our map →

Day 4: San Salvador

Use the capital for history, food, museums, shopping or nightlife. The Historic Center gives a compact introduction to the National Palace, cathedral, theater and renewed public spaces.

Show the Historic Center on our map →

Day 5: Mizata and Los Cóbanos

Start with the quieter surf atmosphere of Mizata, then continue west to Los Cóbanos for volcanic reefs, seafood and marine activities. Diving or boat trips require a qualified local operator and suitable conditions.

Show Mizata → · Show Los Cóbanos →

Transport and timing

  • A rental car, driver or prearranged tourist transport makes this multi-stop route much easier.
  • Do not treat 98 km as the full driving total; lodging choices, transfers and side trips add distance.
  • Leave early for hiking, surfing and marine activities, which depend on weather and local conditions.
  • Confirm park access, surf lessons, guides and boat or dive reservations directly.
