Vehicle choice

Choose for the road, not the photo

A compact car suits main roads and cities. Ask for higher clearance or a real 4×4 before rough mountain access, rural tracks or the rainy season.

Plan places on the map
At the rental counter
  • Photograph every side, wheel, windshield and fuel level.
  • Ask which roads or departments are excluded by the contract.
  • Confirm roadside assistance and accident-reporting instructions.
